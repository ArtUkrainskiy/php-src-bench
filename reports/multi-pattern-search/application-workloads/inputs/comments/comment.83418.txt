This accident defiantly affected our lives, my mentor, my mother, and myself. I miss my horse Santos very much, I may not have been able to meet him until his death in the trailer but my mentor Sue Landusky has been here to help me through this tough time. I just want everyone to know that we are currently going through therapy and are getting our lives back together, very slowly with help of loved  ones and the company of our other horses and mare who survived the accident with a broken tail and nerve damage to her rear end, unfortunately she will never be ridden ever again and will be a pasture mare for the rest of her life. We are emotional and physically drained, my school work has begun to falter because my life is currently shattered pieces, my hope is thin but my heart is what keeps me alive although I wish it could all end.
Sorry for this, but I thought that whoever that read this again should know how the 3 of us are fairing out.
